## Emergency criteria (page Mike directly, no waiting)

- Gas smell or suspected gas leak
- No heat with outdoor temp < 40F
- Burst pipe / water flooding from HVAC equipment
- Carbon monoxide detector alarm
- Smoke or fire from equipment

For any of the above: read the 911 reminder ("if you smell gas, get out of the house and call 911 from outside"), collect address + callback, transfer immediately.
